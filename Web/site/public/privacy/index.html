Privacy Policy

lil health and lil messages · Effective November 25, 2026

lil health and lil messages are discontinued. The service shut down on November 25, 2026, and every account's synced data was deleted from my servers that day. Nothing is collected any more. This policy says what was deleted, what's left on your devices, and what this website does.

lil health and lil messages were made and run by Alexandru Ţurcanu (“I”, “me”), who was the data controller for lil health's service. You can reach me at hello@851.sh.

What was deleted

On November 25, 2026, I deleted everything lil health's servers stored, for every account:

  • Accounts: the identifier from Sign in with Apple or Google, the email address and the name.
  • Devices: the iPhones and Macs signed in to each account, and their sign-in sessions.
  • Health data: every record synced from Apple Health, with the larger files stored beside them, such as workout routes, ECG recordings and clinical documents.
  • Cards: the charts on lil health's Summary.
  • AI agents: each connected agent, and the log of what agents read and changed.
  • Consent records, and the Sign in with Apple tokens kept for accounts.

The databases, the file storage and the servers themselves were deleted, not only emptied, including the copies used for testing. I kept no copy and have no backup, so none of it can be restored, by me or by anyone asking me to.

Cloudflare, Inc., which hosted the service as my processor under its Data Processing Addendum, keeps short-lived request logs, which expire on their own within days, and may keep recovery copies of deleted storage for up to 30 days. I don't use either, and then they're gone.

What's left on your devices

Shutting down the service deleted nothing on your devices:

  • Apple Health on your iPhone is unchanged. lil health only ever read from it.
  • lil health on your iPhone can no longer sign in or upload anything. Deleting the app removes the data it kept on your iPhone. You can also turn off its access in Settings › Privacy & Security › Health.
  • lil health for Mac kept a copy of your Health data on your Mac, which it can no longer update. To remove it, delete the app and the folders ~/Library/Application Support/tech.lil.health and ~/Library/Caches/tech.lil.health.
  • lil messages read your messages on your Mac and nowhere else. It had no account, and it never sent your messages, your contacts or anything about them to my servers. Deleting the app is all there is to do.

Neither app will get another update. The Mac apps still ask GitHub, which hosted their updates, whether there is one, and there's nothing there any more.

Sign in with Apple and Google

lil health may still be listed among the apps that use your Apple Account (Settings › your name › Sign in with Apple) or your Google Account (Third-party apps & services). I no longer hold anything that could use that connection, and you can remove it there. Apple and Google handle it under their own privacy policies.

AI agents

AI agents you connected to your lil health account, such as Claude, Claude Code or ChatGPT, were cut off when the service shut down: the server they read from, mcp.lil.tech, no longer exists. Their connections and the log of what they read were deleted with everything else.

What an agent had already read was sent to its provider's servers (for example Anthropic's or OpenAI's), which handles it under its own terms and privacy policy, not this one. Ask the provider about that. lil.tech/agents explains how to remove lil from your agent.

Agents on your Mac that used the lil command read the copy of your Health data on your Mac, never my servers. Removing that copy, above, ends it.

This website

lil.tech is a few static pages. It has no accounts, cookies, advertising, analytics or tracking. Cloudflare, which hosts it, processes your IP address to deliver the pages, and may keep short-lived request logs. Cloudflare runs data centers around the world, including in the United States.

If you email me, I use your address and what you wrote only to answer you.

Your rights

Because everything was deleted, I hold no personal data from lil health or lil messages about you. There is nothing left to correct or delete, and I can't send you a copy of your data, because none exists. Your Health data is still in Apple Health on your iPhone.

You can still ask me what I hold about you, and have an email you sent me deleted. You also have the right to complain to your data protection authority. I'd appreciate the chance to sort things out first, at hello@851.sh.
